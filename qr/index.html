Snakebite quick guide

Call for help now

Call Philippine Red Cross: 143.

Get medical care now. Do not wait for symptoms.

First aid: Do

  • Move away from the snake. Stay calm and keep the person still.
  • Call for help now.
  • Keep the bitten limb still and avoid unnecessary movement.
  • Take off rings, watches and tight clothing near the bite.
  • Note the time of the bite.
  • Note what the snake looked like, or take a photo only if it is safe.

First aid: Don't

  • Never try to catch or kill the snake.
  • Do not cut the bite or suck out venom.
  • Do not apply ice.
  • Do not tie a tourniquet around the limb; it can be harmful.
  • Do not let the person walk around or drive.
  • Do not wait for symptoms before calling.

Handoff summary

Tell responders the area or landmark, surroundings, whether someone can meet them, bite time, symptoms, and actions taken. Choose Unknown if unsure.
